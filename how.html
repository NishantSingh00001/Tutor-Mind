The method

How your paper gets graded

TutorMind reads your handwriting with a vision AI, marks against the question and your reference, and shows its working so you can catch mistakes it makes.

The steps

  • Transcribe first. The AI reads your photo and writes out what it sees. You get this back as "what I read", so a misread never silently becomes a wrong mark.
  • Mark against the reference. Using the question paper, your book name (with a live web search) or an uploaded PDF, it grades each answer and awards marks out of a maximum.
  • Explain the mistake. Each answer gets specific feedback and the type of mistake — factual slip, incomplete, misread the question, calculation error.
  • Track mastery. Your strengths and gaps per skill are saved on your device and shown on the Progress page.

Free and Pro

Free — all subjects, one answer page per paper, reference by book name + web search, 3 gradings a day.

Pro — upload your own reference PDF for exact grounding, multi-page answer sheets, and unlimited gradings. This is the heavy, token-hungry part, which is why it's gated.

Honest limits — please read

  • Handwriting reading isn't perfect, especially messy writing and Hindi / Devanagari. Always check the "what I read" transcription before trusting a grade.
  • It won't download books for you. Textbooks are copyrighted. Free grading uses the book's name plus web search; for exact text, upload a chapter you're allowed to use (Pro).
  • Whole textbooks are too big for the free tier — there are hard request-size and token limits. Use a chapter or a few pages.
  • Essay and literature marks are subjective. Treat them as structured feedback, not an official grade.
  • Grades are a study aid, not a substitute for your teacher.

Privacy

Your photos are sent only to the grading service to be read and marked, and your mastery record is stored on your device. There are no accounts.
